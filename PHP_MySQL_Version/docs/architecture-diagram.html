NexaCrest Export Ops — Architecture

Four views of the same system: where files physically live, how one document gets assembled and sent, how an order is gated stage by stage, and how a supplier price becomes an order-line price. Reference alongside ARCHITECTURE.md and schema.sql.

1 — Hosting boundary

Only public_html/ is reachable by URL. Application code, uploaded/generated business files, and credentials all sit outside it — on Bluehost and on a local machine alike, since only the base path changes.

2 — Document generation pipeline

Note (13 Nov 2026): the pipeline is unchanged; what it prints grew. Every money document of the order now takes one product table, the shared partial templates/_line_table.html.twig (LineTable, D-339; orders older than migration 2026-11-12g keep the classic table); the Word copy (PHPWord) takes its section numbers from the PDF (D-343); the CI and Packing List print the annexure chosen at Step 11a — Annexure B (as shipped), Annexure A (as quoted) or none (AnnexureChoice, D-336). No mandatory note or label stars on any document (D-352, D-353); headings keep with their first rows (D-354).

One Twig template per document type — no Tier 1 / Tier 2 copies. The payment_presets row assigned to the order is what changes the wording and day-count on Order Confirmation and Commercial Invoice; this is the structural fix for the contamination found in your source documents.

3 — Nine-stage order gate

Note (1 Nov 2026): this drawing is from the original proposal. The stages as built are 1 Enquiry & Quotation · 2 Buyer PO · 3 Proforma Invoice · 4 Order Confirmation · 5 Supplier PO · 6 Freight · 7 Packing & BL · 8 Commercial Invoice & Balance · 9 Despatch & Closure — see ARCHITECTURE.html §G at the repository root and the SOP. Since 1 Nov the Bill of Lading step (10f) also waits for a "before shipment" balance to be cleared and for a Passed pre-shipment inspection (a manager can release it with a written reason). The number of original BLs received is entered as 1 to 10 (not always 3). A document's reviewer is someone other than the person who generated it, unless Users & roles › Self-approval rules allow that person's role (MD and ED by default). Since 2 Nov the COO Prep Sheet can be generated once Stage 8 is open (the BL issued), before the balance clears, and the Export Compliance department works the compliance checklist, the COO Prep Sheet and export benefit claims.

Note (13 Nov 2026): from the first approved Quotation the order's line prices and exchange rate are fixed (D-342); a change to products or prices is an amendment (Amendments › Products & prices), and the order carries on from its current stage. Buyer details follow the client until a document is issued, then only by an approved request (D-337).

Every stage is a hard gate: the next stage stays locked until its condition is met, and every gate condition below comes from your spec's Business Rules (Section 15), not from application guesswork.

4 — Supplier price to order line (13 Nov 2026)

Owner decisions D-359 to D-365 and D-342. A product is priced from its own configured base price, not straight from a supplier: suppliers give offers, a manager configures the product once, and the approved Quotation fixes what the buyer was offered. Nothing in this chain changes a price by itself.
