Rules for every order

  • Work top to bottom. A step opens only when the one before it is done; the current step is outlined in gold on the order page.
  • Every document follows the same path: Generate → Review & Approve → Send (or mark sent when email is off). Until it has, the step's button stays greyed out with a note saying what the document still needs.
  • Skip a step only with a real reason. Not Applicable fills its reason for you; Skipped always needs one typed.
  • Supplier and buyer never cross: no buyer name, price or document ever goes to a supplier, and the other way round.
  • CAFIN (internal financial annexure) is for MD / ED / Admin, Accounts and the CA only, and never goes to the client — not by email, link, download or portal.
  • Record every money receipt with that day's exchange rate, and every payment with its date, mode and UTR. Reference numbers use the 2-digit year (SC/CI/26/…).
  • Vendor bills: attach the bill, say whether it is a proforma or the tax invoice, pick GST as a % — the amount and total fill in themselves. A bill can be paid in parts.
  • Talk to the client through Order Communication on the order page so the history stays with the order.
