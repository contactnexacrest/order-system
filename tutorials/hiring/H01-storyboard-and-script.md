# H01 — Become a NexaCrest Associate (hiring video)

**Status:** DRAFT 3 storyboard and script for the owner's review. Nothing is recorded yet.
**Language:** English only (D-419). **Length:** about 11 minutes.
**Look and voice:** the same as videos C01–C15: title cards, chapter cards, the associate portal on screen with a
pointer, captions at the bottom, and the same narrator voice.

## How the words are written (owner, 25 Nov 2026)

- **Very simple, like for a 4th grader.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Everyday words.
- **No room for doubt.** Every rule is said plainly, and the hard ones get a small example.
- **Not every detail, but enough that nobody can argue later.** Everything said matches what the app already does.
- **Every number is an example** (D-415). Each time numbers appear, the screen and the voice say:
  *"Example numbers only. These are not NexaCrest's real rates. Your real numbers are in your signed agreement."*

## Rules this video follows (owner decisions)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| All numbers are made-up, round examples. No real rate, minimum value, number of days, price or business figure. | D-415 |
| Commission = a % of the **FOB value of the goods on the order**. Turned into rupees at the rate at which the buyer's money reached us, then fixed. We pay that rupee amount, and only that. | D-415, D-425 |
| Paid only by bank transfer into the associate's own checked account. No cheques mentioned. | D-416 |
| Never mention NexaCrest's margin. | D-425 |
| "NexaCrest Group is an export company in India." Prices come from the associate's portal. | D-425 |
| Levels count **orders**, not clients. The exact counting rules are explained with examples. | D-410, D-425 |
| First order always earns. Later orders earn only at or above the minimum order value. | D-410, D-425 |
| **No commission on an order cancelled before full payment.** A paid commission is never taken back (except fraud or breach of the agreement). | D-245, D-410 |
| Nothing to pay to join; no export experience needed (an advantage if you have it); the buyer belongs to the associate whose link or code they used. | D-424 |
| Why we ask for bank details and bank proof. | D-417, D-418 |
| Portal words: no "ladder", "basis", "payout run" or "protection". | D-330 |
| No date mentioned in the order part (3.3). | D-425 |
| Nothing internal shown: no real client, supplier, order, cost, CAFIN, business totals or staff screens. | D-415, D-217 |

**Recording set-up:** a demo associate whose demo rates are the example rates (10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 %),
demo buyers "TEST Example Buyer 1/2/3", demo products with example prices. No real names, e-mails or phone
numbers. Demo data removed afterwards. The end card shows `[contact details added by NexaCrest]`.

**Example card** (shown with every number): *Example numbers only. These are not NexaCrest's real rates. Your real numbers are in your signed agreement.*

## Notes for the owner

1. **Your agreement must state** the minimum order value, the number of days for orders that count as one, and
   how long a sign-up lasts without an order. The video says all three are "in your agreement" (D-415: no real
   numbers in the video).
2. **4.8, "the buyer's first payment has reached our bank":** with a Letter of Credit it counts once the LC is
   confirmed. The video leaves the LC out to keep it simple (most buyers pay an advance). Say if you want it in.
3. **4.10, the rate fixed at the Proforma Invoice:** this is how the app works today (D-410, D-245).
4. **Dates:** part 3 has no date (D-425). Part 5 says "once a month" and the closing-day rule (D-421), and the
   payments page shows "Will be paid to you" without reading out a date.
5. **Owner's review of 25 Nov 2026** (D-424, D-425) is applied: nothing to pay, export experience, two people
   with the same buyer, no margin, "no commission on an order cancelled before full payment", NexaCrest Group,
   prices from the portal, FOB value of the goods on the order, we always pay the fixed rupee amount, orders not
   clients, the minimum order value and the number of days, very simple language.
