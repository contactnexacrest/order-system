# H01 — Become a NexaCrest Associate (hiring video)

**Status:** DRAFT storyboard and script for the owner's review. Nothing is recorded (task #122).
**Language:** English only (D-419). **Length:** about 9–10 minutes.
**Look and voice:** the same as videos C01–C15: title cards, chapter cards, the associate portal on screen with
a pointer, captions at the bottom, and the same narrator voice.

## Rules this video follows (owner decisions)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| Every number is an obvious example. Each time numbers appear, the caption and narration say: *"Example numbers for illustration only — not NexaCrest's rates. Your actual commission is set out in your signed agreement."* | D-415 |
| Commission = a % of the **FOB value of the goods on the order**, turned into rupees at the exchange rate at which the client paid us, then fixed; that rupee amount is what is paid. | D-415 |
| Paid only by bank transfer into the associate's own checked account. No cheques mentioned anywhere. | D-416 |
| 8 chapters; about 10 doubts answered; no contact details (placeholder only, the owner adds them when sending). | D-417 |
| Why we ask for bank details and bank proof, as on the portal's bank form. | D-417, D-418 |
| Plain words, as in the portal: no "ladder", "basis", "payout run" or "protection" in what the associate hears or reads. Use "your level", "your rate", "payment date", "your client". | D-330 |
| Earned once the buyer has paid in full; a paid commission is never taken back (except fraud or breach of the agreement); **no commission on an order cancelled before full payment**. | D-245, D-410 |
| Paid on the monthly payment date: buyer's payment cleared by the month's closing day → paid that month, otherwise the next month. | D-421 |
| Nothing internal shown: no real client, supplier, order, price, cost, margin, CAFIN, business totals or staff screens. | D-415, D-217 |

**Recording set-up (so no real rate can show on screen):** a demo associate ("Demo Associate", demo buyers
"TEST Example Buyer 1/2/3") whose commission rule in the demo database is set to the example rates
10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 %, with example order values (USD 10,000, USD 20,000 …). The demo data is removed
afterwards. No real names, e-mails or phone numbers (PII rule); the end card shows `[contact details added by NexaCrest]`.
