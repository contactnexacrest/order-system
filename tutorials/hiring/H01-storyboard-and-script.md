# H01 — Become a NexaCrest Associate (hiring video)

**Status:** APPROVED by the owner (25 Nov 2026, draft 4). To be recorded in English after the push.
**Language:** English only (D-419). **Length:** about 12 minutes.
**Look and voice:** the same as videos C01–C15 (title cards, chapter cards, the associate portal with a pointer,
captions, the same narrator voice), plus simple story drawings (a map, a calendar, a growing stack of coins).

## How the words are written (owner, 25 Nov 2026)

- **A story.** The video talks to the viewer as "you" and follows one simple story: you bring one buyer, the buyer
  keeps ordering, and you keep earning. Then you bring more buyers.
- **It motivates.** One client can pay you again and again, on every order they place that meets the minimum order value, for as long as your
  agreement runs. There is no limit to how much you can earn: the more business you bring, the more you earn.
- **Very simple, like for a 4th grader.** Short sentences, everyday words, one idea at a time.
- **No room for doubt.** Every rule is said plainly, with a small example. Not every detail, but enough that
  nobody can argue later. Everything said matches what the app already does.
- **Every number is an example** (D-415). Each time numbers appear, the screen and the voice say:
  *"Example numbers only. These are not NexaCrest's real rates. Your real numbers are in your signed agreement."*
- No real names: the story uses "you", "your buyer" and "a shop in another country".

## Rules this video follows (owner decisions)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| All numbers are made-up, round examples. No real rate, minimum value, number of days, price or business figure. | D-415 |

| Paid only by bank transfer into the associate's own checked account. No cheques mentioned. | D-416 |

Prices come from the associate's portal. | D-425, D-430 |

## Notes for the owner

1. **Your agreement must state** the minimum order value, the number of days for orders that count as one, and
   how long a sign-up lasts without an order. The video says all three are "in your agreement" (D-415: no real
   numbers in the video).
2. **5.3, "the buyer's first payment has reached our bank":** for a Letter of Credit order, it counts once the LC
   is confirmed. Left out to keep it simple; say if you want it in.
3. **4.3 and 4.6** are the motivating part: one client ordering four times a year (example), and "no limit".
   Nothing in the app limits an associate's commission.
4. **5.5, the rate fixed at the Proforma Invoice:** how the app works today (D-410, D-245).
5. **Dates:** the order part has no date (D-425); part 6 says "once a month" and the closing-day rule (D-421).
6. **Small disclaimer (owner, 25 Nov 2026; no "today", so it never dates):** only at the very end, small and friendly — "This video gives a simple picture of how things work. Your signed agreement is what counts, and NexaCrest may update its terms from time to time." On screen in small type, and one calm spoken sentence. It protects NexaCrest if terms change later, without putting the viewer off.
7. **5.8 corrected after approval (25 Nov 2026):** under D-292 (11 Nov 2026) the associate's portal shows only the current %, never levels, ladder or counts. So 5.8 now says the portal shows "your current rate" (not "your level" and "how many more orders"). The video still explains levels and counting (D-425), as the owner asked.
8. **Owner's review of 25 Nov 2026** (D-424, D-425) is applied: nothing to pay, export experience, two people
   with the same buyer, no margin, "no commission on an order cancelled before full payment", NexaCrest Group,
   prices from the portal, FOB value of the goods on the order, we always pay the fixed rupee amount, orders not
   clients, the minimum order value and the number of days, very simple language, told as a story that motivates.
