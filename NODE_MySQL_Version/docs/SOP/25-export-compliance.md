# 25 — Export Compliance: government and agency documents

Export Compliance is the department for the 1–3 people who deal with the
government and outside agencies on every order: ECGC cover, the Certificate
of Origin, phytosanitary, fumigation and inspection certificates, export
benefit claims (RoDTEP, Duty Drawback …) and the eBRC / FIRC from the bank.
They collect what the agencies need from the other departments, submit it,
and bring the certificates back onto the order. (Owner decision D-226,
2 Nov 2026.)

## 1. Roles

Like Procurement, Logistics and Finance, the department has a ladder of
three designations, each with its own role. Admin → Departments assigns
people to them; **Log in as** switches into the department like any other.

| Designation | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Export Compliance Executive** | Works the orders assigned to them: the compliance checklist, the COO Prep Sheet, export benefit eligibility and claims, government documents on the order, their own task list. |
| **Export Compliance Manager** | Everything above on **every** order; picks the department's owner for an order; reviews and approves the department's documents; edits the list of compliance tasks (Admin → Compliance task types). |
| **Export Compliance Director** | Everything the Manager has, plus the management cockpit. |

## 2. What they see — and what they never see

They see the order, its documents and its **selling (invoice) values** —
the Certificate of Origin and the benefit claims need them.

They **never** see: purchase prices or supplier bills, logistics costs
(CHA, transport, freight), what the buyer has paid or still owes, the
order's profit (Step 12), incentives or associate commission, or the CA's
annexure (CAFIN). Those parts of the order page are simply not shown, and
the matching downloads are refused.

## 3. Daily work

**My tasks** (top menu) is the department's queue, built from the orders
themselves — nothing to hand over by hand:

| Task | Appears when | Where to do it |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance checklist item (ECGC, pre-shipment inspection, fumigation, phytosanitary, due diligence …) | Order-phase items once the advance has cleared; shipment-phase items once Step 10 (packing & BL) opens | Order page → **Compliance Checklist** (by Step 7 and by Step 10) |
| Apply for the Certificate of Origin | Stage 8 opens (the BL is issued) — before the balance is cleared | Order page → **Step 13a · Generate COO Prep Sheet** |
| Answer export benefit eligibility | The BL is issued and a scheme is still Pending | Order page → **Step 13g** → *Which schemes is this order eligible for?* |
| File the export benefit claim | The order is complete, the balance remittance has landed and an eligible scheme has no claim yet | Order page → **Step 13g** → *File a claim* |
| Get the eBRC / FIRC from the bank | An advance or balance payment has cleared and its FIRC / eBRC reference is not recorded yet | Ask the bank; give the reference to Accounts, who record it in the CA module |

Each task has standard days and escalation days (Admin → Task due days),
like every other department's.

## 4. On the order page

- **Compliance Checklist** — mark each item Done, Pending approval, or
  Not applicable with a reason. Everyone who could do this before (the
  roles with *Close orders*) still can.
- **Step 13a · COO Prep Sheet** — **Generate COO Prep Sheet**. This is the
  only document Export Compliance generates; every other document is
  generated by the department that owns it.
- **Step 13g · Government Benefits** — answer each scheme's eligibility
  (never leave it Pending), then file each claim: scheme, shipping bill /
  scroll no., amount claimed, date filed. **The money received** is
  recorded by Accounts & Finance when it lands.
- **Step 14 · Additional Documents** — attach the ECGC policy, the
  Certificate of Origin, the phytosanitary / fumigation / inspection
  certificates, the eBRC … with a clear title. Removing an attached file
  stays with *Manage orders*.

## 5. Permissions (Admin → Roles & Permissions)

| Permission | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Compliance checklist | Work the order's compliance checklist (split out of *Close orders*; every role that had *Close orders* keeps it). |
| File export benefit claims | Answer scheme eligibility and file claims, without *Manage order financials*. |
| Generate compliance documents | Generate the COO Prep Sheet, without the general *Generate documents*. |
| Attach documents to an order | Attach files in Step 14, without *Manage orders*. |

The money permissions stay as they are: the department holds *See selling
prices* only.

## 6. Not given (and why)

- **BL Instruction** — it carries logistics costs, so Logistics generates
  it; Export Compliance reads the shipment details from the order.
- **Recording the eBRC / FIRC reference and benefit money received** — they
  sit next to the rupee amounts actually credited, so Accounts & Finance
  record them.

## 7. ECGC cover, eBRC follow-up and claim deadlines (2 Nov 2026, gap-list Q-6)

**ECGC credit limit (buyer page → "ECGC credit limit").** Record the limit ECGC
approved for the buyer: amount and currency, approved on, valid until, the ECGC
reference and the approval letter. The company's own ECGC policy (number, cover
%, valid until, the declaration day) is in **Admin → Company Settings →
compliance**. The buyer page and the order page compare the limit with the
buyer's open orders (order value not yet received) and warn when:

- the open orders are above the limit — "the amount above the limit is not covered";
- the limit has expired;
- there is an ECGC policy but no limit recorded for the buyer.

Creating a new order for such a buyer shows the same warning; the order is
still created. People who may not see selling prices see the warning without
amounts.

**Shipment declaration (order page → "ECGC cover").** Every shipment is
declared to ECGC by the 10th of the month after the BL date. Record the date
and ECGC's declaration reference there. Export Compliance Managers get a
reminder once the BL month is over, and again when the 10th has passed.

**Missing eBRC / FIRC.** Marking an order fully settled never waits for an
eBRC. Step 13h lists each payment still without a FIRC / eBRC reference, and
**Reports → Missing eBRC / FIRC** (with CSV) lists them for all orders, with
the days since the money arrived; amounts show only to people who see buyer
payments. On the 1st of each month Finance and the Export Compliance Managers
get one reminder with the count. Ask the bank for the reference and hand it to
Accounts, who record it.

**Incentive claims (order page → "Incentive claims — tracking").** Each claim
shows claimed, **sanctioned** and received amounts, the **claim deadline** and
a **status** (Filed / Sanctioned / Received / Rejected). The deadline defaults
from Admin → Company Settings per scheme, counted from the shipping bill date
(RoDTEP and RoSCTL 365 days; Duty Drawback none, as it is paid through the
shipping bill) — **to be confirmed with the CA**; change it on the claim when
the scheme says otherwise. Reminders go out 30 and 7 days before the deadline.
"Received" is set automatically when Accounts record the money.
