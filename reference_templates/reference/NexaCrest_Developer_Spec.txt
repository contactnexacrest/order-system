LEGAL TERMS BOX — 6 CLAUSES IN THIS EXACT ORDER:

  Cancellation:
    Orders may not be cancelled after production commences. The Advance Payment
    is non-refundable once production commences. In the event of cancellation
    after production commences, NexaCrest's total recovery shall not exceed the
    Advance Payment plus 20% of the total FOB Order value. The parties agree this
    represents a genuine pre-estimate of NexaCrest's unrecoverable production
    costs, overhead, opportunity loss, and market price risk.

  Limitation of Liability:
    NexaCrest's maximum liability under this document shall not exceed the total
    FOB Order value stated herein. NexaCrest is not liable for any indirect,
    consequential, or punitive damages, or for loss of profit or business.

  Production Timeline:
    The estimated shipment timeline stated in this document is indicative only
    and does not constitute a guaranteed delivery date. NexaCrest will make every
    effort to meet the estimated timeline. NexaCrest will notify the Buyer in
    writing within 5 Business Days of becoming aware of any delay to production
    or shipment, and will provide a revised estimated timeline within 10 Business
    Days of such notification.

  Re-inspection and Loading:
    NexaCrest shall load only the confirmed passed pieces. Confirmed failed pieces
    shall be excluded from the shipment. The Commercial Invoice and all payment
    obligations shall be adjusted to reflect only the pieces actually loaded and
    shipped. Neither Party shall have any further claim in respect of the excluded
    pieces.

  Structural Defect Claims:
    NexaCrest confirms to the best of its knowledge that Products are free from
    structural defects present at Loading and not detectable at Pre-loading
    Inspection. No claim accepted for: (a) conditions detectable at Pre-loading
    Inspection; (b) damage from Buyer's processing, cutting, handling, or
    installation; or (c) natural stone characteristics. Any claim must be raised
    by the Buyer in writing with photographic evidence within 14 Calendar Days of
    confirmed receipt at Buyer's premises. No claim accepted after this period.

  Governing Law and Dispute Resolution:
    This document is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute shall first be
    notified in writing by email or WhatsApp. Both parties shall attempt amicable
    resolution within 45 Calendar Days of written notice. If unresolved, the
    dispute shall be referred to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and
    Conciliation Act, 1996. Seat: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Language: English.
    The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods
    is expressly excluded from this Agreement.

  CRITICAL NOTE: The phrase MUST be "is expressly excluded from this Agreement" —
  NOT "does not apply". "Does not apply" reads as a characterisation of current law.
  "Is expressly excluded" is the parties' deliberate contractual choice and is
  legally stronger in all jurisdictions. Never substitute "does not apply".

SECTION 9: LEGAL LANGUAGE RULES — NEVER DEVIATE
================================================================================

The following substitutions were applied during the legal language audit and
MUST be preserved in all documents and in the app template engine:

  NEVER USE              → USE INSTEAD
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  "will notify"          → "will notify within 5 Business Days of becoming aware"
  "NexaCrest warrants"   → "NexaCrest confirms to the best of its knowledge"
  "must be raised"       → "must be raised by the Buyer"
  "immediately after"    → "within 24 hours of"
  "Capitalised terms"    → "Terms used" or "The following terms"
  "reasonable effort"    → "every effort" (drop "reasonable")
  "reasonable efforts"   → specific timeframe (5 Business Days / 10 Business Days)
  "warrants" (any form)  → "confirms to the best of its knowledge"
  "guarantees"           → never use — use "estimates" or "endeavours"
  "ensures"              → "endeavours to ensure" or rewrite specifically

  ALWAYS use:
  - Specific numbers for all timeframes (days, not "promptly" or "immediately")
  - Named party for every obligation ("by the Buyer", "by NexaCrest")
  - "genuine pre-estimate" language for liquidated damages clauses
  - Governing law = India, Seat = Bengaluru, Language = English, CISG excluded
