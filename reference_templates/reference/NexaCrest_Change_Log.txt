HOW TO READ THIS LOG
----------------------------------------
DOCUMENT: which file was changed
SECTION:  where in the document
TYPE:     ADD (new content), REPLACE (old to new), FIX (error correction)
OLD:      exact text before change (or 'not present' for ADDs)
NEW:      exact text after change
COUNT:    how many times this replacement occurred
NOTE:     reason for change

IMPORTANT FOR APP DEVELOPERS:
- All ADD changes require inserting new content into the T&C section
- The Definitions block goes FIRST in T&C, before all existing clauses
- The Legal Terms block goes AFTER Definitions, before existing clauses
- Template brackets [like this] must remain — filled at runtime
- Internal notes marked [INTERNAL NOTE] must be REMOVED before sending to buyer

      In this document, "NexaCrest International Private Limited" is referred to
      as "NexaCrest". Both names refer to the same legal entity.

  A2  T/T (Telegraphic Transfer):
      an electronic bank wire transfer sent directly from the Buyer's bank to
      NexaCrest's designated bank account at State Bank of India as stated in
      this document.

  A3  Payment Cleared:
      the relevant payment confirmed as actually credited to NexaCrest's bank
      account by NexaCrest's accounts team. A Remittance Copy alone does not
      constitute Payment Cleared.

  A4  Remittance Copy:
      the bank transfer receipt or SWIFT confirmation sent by the Buyer as
      evidence of transfer. Receipt of a Remittance Copy by NexaCrest does not
      constitute Payment Cleared.

  A5  Advance Payment:
      the advance percentage of the FOB Order value paid by T/T before production
      commences, as stated in this document.

  A6  Balance Payment:
      the remaining FOB Order value after the Advance Payment, paid by T/T as per
      the payment trigger stated in this document.

  A7  Debit Note:
      a written demand issued by NexaCrest for any agreed cost additional to the
      FOB Order value, including but not limited to freight, cargo insurance,
      inspection fees, or certificate fees. Each Debit Note must be confirmed as
      Payment Cleared before NexaCrest proceeds with the related activity.

  A8  Loading:
      the physical act of stuffing Products into the container at NexaCrest's
      designated processing facility. Loading is distinct from Shipment.

  A9  Shipment:
      the departure of the vessel from the port of loading. Shipment is distinct
      from Loading and from Delivery.

  A10 FCL (Full Container Load):
      a shipment occupying one complete container, approximately 24-27 MT
      including packing. Minimum order quantity is 1 FCL.

  A11 Production Commences:
      the point at which NexaCrest begins manufacturing the Products for this
      Order. Production commences only after the Advance Payment is confirmed
      as Payment Cleared. The Advance Payment is non-refundable from this point.

  A12 Shipment Readiness Confirmation:
      a written notification from NexaCrest by email or WhatsApp confirming that
      the Products have passed quality inspection (NexaCrest's internal quality
      check, or third-party Pre-loading Inspection where applicable), are fully
      packed, and are ready for vessel booking.
      NOTE: Sequence is critical — inspected FIRST, packed SECOND, ready THIRD.

REASON: A 2-row table loses its top border on the content row when the table

to lose — the border stays intact on all sides at all times regardless of

ADDENDUM SECTION 11 — LEGAL LANGUAGE FIXES APPLIED TO ALL 18 DOCUMENTS
SESSION: October 2026 — Comprehensive Legal Language Audit
================================================================================

A full legal language audit was conducted on all 18 buyer-facing documents.
The following changes were applied globally — every fix below is present in
all 18 documents unless a specific document is named.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11.1  PRODUCTION TIMELINE CLAUSE — COMPLETE REWRITE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOCATION: Section 8, Legal Terms box, "Production Timeline" bullet

OLD TEXT:
  "...NexaCrest will make every reasonable effort to meet the estimated timeline.
  NexaCrest will use reasonable efforts to notify the Buyer in writing at the
  earliest opportunity if production or shipment is delayed."

NEW TEXT:
  "...NexaCrest will make every effort to meet the estimated timeline. NexaCrest
  will notify the Buyer in writing within 5 Business Days of becoming aware of
  any delay to production or shipment, and will provide a revised estimated
  timeline within 10 Business Days of such notification."

REASON:
  "Reasonable effort" is vague — creates argument about what "reasonable" means.
  "At the earliest opportunity" is vague — no enforceable timeframe.
  Replaced with specific, measurable obligations:
  - 5 Business Days to notify from date of awareness (not from delay event)
  - 10 Business Days to provide revised timeline
  Clock starts from NexaCrest becoming AWARE — not from when delay occurred,
  since delays in supply chain may not be immediately known.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11.2  STRUCTURAL DEFECT CLAIMS — WARRANTY LANGUAGE REMOVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOCATION: Section 8, Legal Terms box, "Structural Defect Claims" bullet

OLD TEXT:
  "NexaCrest warrants Products are free from structural defects..."

NEW TEXT:
  "NexaCrest confirms to the best of its knowledge that Products are free from
  structural defects..."

REASON:
  "Warrants" creates an absolute guarantee in law — strict liability if any
  defect exists regardless of detectability. "Confirms to the best of its
  knowledge" is protective of the buyer while not creating an absolute warranty
  NexaCrest cannot guarantee across a full supply chain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11.3  CLAIM OBLIGATION — PARTY NAMED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOCATION: Section 8, Legal Terms box, "Structural Defect Claims" bullet

OLD TEXT:
  "Any claim must be raised in writing with photographic evidence..."

NEW TEXT:
  "Any claim must be raised by the Buyer in writing with photographic evidence..."

REASON:
  "Must be raised" with no named party is ambiguous. A lawyer could argue
  NexaCrest has an obligation to raise it on the buyer's behalf. "By the Buyer"
  makes the obligation owner unambiguous.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11.4  IMMEDIATE PAYMENT NOTIFICATION — VAGUE TIMEFRAME FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOCATION: 06_DebitNote.docx (both tiers) — remittance copy instruction

OLD TEXT:
  "...send remittance copy...immediately after payment."

NEW TEXT:
  "...send remittance copy...within 24 hours of payment."

REASON:
  "Immediately" is legally vague and practically unenforceable in international
  transactions across time zones. 24 hours is specific and measurable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11.5  DEFINITION TRIGGER — FORMATTING DEPENDENCY REMOVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOCATION: Reference line near top of every document AND intro line in
          Definitions box

OLD REFERENCE LINE:
  "Capitalised terms used in this document are defined in Section 8..."

NEW REFERENCE LINE:
  "Terms used in this document are defined in Section 8 — Legal Terms &
  Definitions at the end of this document."

OLD DEFINITIONS INTRO:
  "Capitalised terms used throughout this document are defined below."

NEW DEFINITIONS INTRO:
  "The following terms have the meanings given below wherever they appear
  in this document."

REASON:
  "Capitalised terms" creates a formatting-dependent trigger. If any defined
  term appears in lowercase anywhere in the document body, a lawyer can argue
  the definition does not apply to that instance. The new wording makes the
  definition apply by virtue of being listed — not by how the word looks in
  the body. No formatting dependency. No argument possible.
